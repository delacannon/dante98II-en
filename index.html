    How to patch

    Drop your .hdi or .nhd in the box above. You get a patched copy ending in -en. Your original file is not changed.

    Or from a terminal (Node 18+):
    node apply.mjs Dante98II.hdi
    node apply.mjs path\to\DANTE2 (patches the folder, keeps backups as .orig)

    The .bps files in src/ also work with Flips or any other BPS patcher.

    How to play

    Open the patched image in np21w, T98-Next or anyPC98 and reset.

    Problems

    Not the original file: different version of the game, or already modified. Nothing was changed.

    Start a new game. Japanese saves may not load right.
